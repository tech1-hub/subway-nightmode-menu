Why Menu-Based Customization Works

Customizing your order by thinking in menu components is the fastest way to get a meal that matches taste, budget and dietary needs. Rather than random swaps, use a consistent order flow: choose bread → pick protein → add cheese & veggies → select sauces → decide size & side.

Menu Components (Quick Reference)

  • Bread / Base: Footlong, 6-inch, flatbread, wrap, or bowl.
  • Protein: Chicken, turkey, ham, tuna, steak, plant-based options.
  • Cheese: American, cheddar, provolone, feta (if available).
  • Veggies: Lettuce, tomato, cucumber, green peppers, onions, spinach, olives.
  • Sauces: Mayo, ranch, chipotle, sweet onion, mustard, oil & vinegar.
  • Add-ons: Avocado, extra meat, extra cheese.

Step-by-Step: Build Your Custom Subway Order

Step 1 — Pick the Right Base

Decide how you want to eat the meal: bread for satiety, bowl for low-carb, wrap for portability. Here’s a simple table to compare base types at a glance.

Base Comparison
BaseMain BenefitWhen to Choose
6-inchSmaller portion, lower priceLight appetite, controlled calories
FootlongDouble portion, shareableHungry, value for money
WrapCompact, less bread chewOn-the-go eating
BowlLower carbs, high veggiesLow-carb or gluten-free
FlatbreadSoft texture, slightly different carb profilePreference for taste/texture

Step 2 — Choose Protein Based on Goals

Proteins dictate flavor and calories. Use this simple guide when selecting:

  • Lean & Low-Calorie: Turkey, chicken breast.
  • Higher Protein & Flavor: Steak, double meat, tuna.
  • Vegetarian: Veggie patty (where available), cheese & avocado combos.

Step 3 — Add Cheese and Veg Smartly

Cheese increases calories and fat — choose thin-sliced or skip if you want lighter meals. Veggies add bulk and micronutrients with few calories.

Step 4 — Sauce Strategy

Sauces can dramatically change calorie counts. Use the table below to match sauces with goals.

Sauce Guide
SauceProfileTip

MustardLow-calorie, sharpGood with ham/turkey
RanchCreamy, high-calorieUse sparingly or on the side
Chipotle SouthwestSmoky, spicy, richerGreat for bold sandwiches — request light
MayoCreamy, high-calBest used as a small spread

Step 5 — Choose Sides and Drinks

Sides determine the meal’s final calorie and price. Substitute chips for a baked option or a fruit cup when available. Opt for water or unsweetened tea to cut calories and sugar.

Pricing & Budgeting: How Choices Affect Cost

Price changes by region and add-ons. As a general rule:

  • Upgrading to footlong roughly doubles base price.
  • Double meat and premium add-ons (avocado, extra cheese) add a noticeable surcharge.
  • Bowls and wraps may be priced slightly differently depending on the location.

Customization Tips & Shortcuts

  • Ask for sauces on the side: puts you in control of quantity.
  • Toast selectively: to add texture without extra calories.
  • Mix hot & cold: hot meats with fresh veggies balance texture.
  • Split a footlong: order two different halves to compare or share.
  • Use cheese as a topping, not an option: ask for "light cheese" to reduce fat.

Order Flow Examples (What to Say)

Use these short scripts to order confidently at the counter or kiosk:

  1. Simple/Light: "6-inch turkey on multigrain, no cheese, extra spinach and tomatoes, mustard on the side."
  2. Flavorful: "Footlong chicken teriyaki on Italian herbs & cheese, provolone, add jalapeños, light chipotle."
  3. Low-carb: "Make it a bowl with double chicken, extra veggies, avocado and oil & vinegar."

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I mix sauces?

Yes — but mixing many sauces increases calories and muddles flavors. A recommended approach is one primary sauce plus a splash of a second (e.g., oil & vinegar + mustard).

What if I need allergen info?

Always ask staff for the allergen guide at your location. Cross-contamination is possible on shared equipment. For ingredient-level clarity, refer to documented nutrition pages or onsite menu charts.
